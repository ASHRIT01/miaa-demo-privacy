MIAA Demo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

MIAA Demo is a test app. It answers messages sent to a demo Instagram account and a demo Facebook Page with an automated assistant, so a salon chat assistant can be tried out before it is used for real. Only a few invited testers use it. This page explains what data it handles and what happens to that data.

What we collect

When you message the demo Instagram account or demo Facebook Page, the app receives:

  • the messages you send, and the replies sent to you;
  • the name and the account or page-scoped ID that Instagram or Facebook shares with the app;
  • details you choose to type, such as a service, date, time or your name for a booking request.

We don't collect passwords or payment details, and we don't read anything outside the conversation with the demo account.

How we use it

  • To reply to your messages automatically.
  • To pass the conversation to a person when the assistant can't help.
  • To test and improve how the assistant answers.

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell it.

Who processes it

  • Meta (Instagram and Facebook) delivers the messages. Meta's own privacy policy applies to your use of its apps.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model that writes the replies. Your messages in the conversation are sent to it to write each reply.
  • Conversations are stored in a chat inbox (Chatwoot) that we run ourselves, and in a small database used by the assistant.

How long we keep it

Conversations are kept only for the demo. They are deleted when the demo ends, or sooner if you ask.

Deleting your data

To have your conversation and details deleted, email ashrit0104@gmail.com. We delete your data within 7 days and reply to confirm.
